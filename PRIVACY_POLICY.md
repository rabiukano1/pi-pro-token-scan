# Privacy Policy for PIPRO QR

**Last Updated: August 26, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **PIPRO QR** ("we", "our", or "the App"), with package identifier `com.piproqr`, handles user data and privacy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe, transparent experience.

---

## 1. Summary — We Respect Your Privacy
- **PIPRO QR DOES NOT collect, store, or transmit your private keys or seed phrases.**
- **PIPRO QR DOES NOT require you to create an account or provide personal identity information.**
- All QR code generation and verification takes place directly on your device.

---

## 2. Information We Access & How It Is Used

### A. Camera Permission (`android.permission.CAMERA`)
- **Purpose**: The App requests camera access solely to enable the QR code scanning feature in real time.
- **Data Handling**: Camera frames are processed entirely on-device using local optical scanning libraries to detect Solana Pay QR codes. No photos, videos, or camera recordings are ever saved, stored, or transmitted to any server.

### B. Photos & Storage (`android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / Gallery Access)
- **Purpose**: The App allows you to select a saved QR code image from your device gallery to scan or share generated QR cards.
- **Data Handling**: The App only accesses the specific image you choose. No other media or files on your device are read or uploaded.

### C. Public Blockchain Data
- **Purpose**: When checking your PIPRO token balance or verifying transactions, the App queries public Solana Mainnet RPC endpoints (`https://api.mainnet-beta.solana.com`).
- **Data Handling**: Only public wallet addresses entered by the user are queried to retrieve public on-chain balances and transaction signatures. No private wallet credentials are ever accessed.

## 4. Data Security
Because PIPRO QR does not operate user accounts or external database servers, your wallet addresses and generated QR codes remain strictly on your local device. We never have access to your cryptocurrency funds or private keys.
