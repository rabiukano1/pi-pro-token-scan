Privacy Policy for PIPRO QR

Effective Date: August 26, 2026

Package Name: com.piproqr

This Privacy Policy explains how PIPRO QR ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparent information regarding device permissions and data processing.

1. Summary — Zero Personal Data Collection

  • No Private Key Access: PIPRO QR never collects, stores, transmits, or asks for your private keys or seed recovery phrases.
  • No Account Needed: The App requires no sign-up, email, phone number, or personal identity verification.
  • Local Processing: All QR generation and barcode validation occurs locally on your mobile device.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

A. Camera (android.permission.CAMERA)

The App requests camera access solely to enable real-time QR code scanning of Solana Pay merchant and member cards. Camera data is processed completely on-device using local optical recognition. No video or photos are saved or uploaded to external servers.

B. Gallery / Media Storage

The App allows you to optionally select a QR image from your photo gallery or save a generated payment card image. The App only accesses the single file you select.

C. Public Blockchain Information

When using the "Check Token Balance" or "Last Received" feature, the App queries public Solana Mainnet RPC endpoints to retrieve on-chain token balances and public transaction receipts for the public address you enter.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development, PIPRO QR integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner and interstitial advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as Advertising IDs), device information, and diagnostics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps

4. Data Security

Because PIPRO QR operates without central user databases, your wallet addresses and generated QR records remain strictly within your device's memory.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not targeted to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
